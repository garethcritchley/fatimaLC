Who are you?

"I am not who you think I am, I am not who I think I am, I am who I think, you think, I am." Charles Horton Cooley

What is the first question you get asked when you meet someone in a social gathering? So often, we are defined by our profession, financial status, marital status, whether we have children or not, our appearance and other superficial attributes. Do you find you dedicate your day to day life trying to meet other people's expectations?

BE YOUR AUTHENTIC SELF

Setbacks are a part of life.

Losing a loved one, having a marriage breakdown, being fired from a job, any of these major events can have you feeling lost and not knowing who you are or who you want to be.

If you have been through a major life event and want to work on navigating through life, I am here to help you find out how to be your true self, unapologetic and proud.

The first step is to discover how you define success in life as opposed to how others perceive success on your behalf. Connecting with your true self isn't always simple, a coach can help you if you both resonate with each other. A coach is who will guide you to finding yourself.

If you worked hard to get to where you are in life, yet you still feel lost and something doesn't feel right but you don't know what it is, book in a discovery call with me. Together we can find what's holding you back from being your authentic self.
